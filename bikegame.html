📜 Harley Highway 100 Official Regulations

Standardized Grand Prix rules for Drivers and Spectators.

Rule 1: Strict Role Separation

A participant is strictly either a Driver or a Spectator. Drivers stake ₹10 to enter their bike and are strictly exempted from betting on any rider. Only Spectators place variable bets (₹10 to ₹100) to win the 80% jackpot.

Rule 2: Tachometer & Redline Bonus

Rolling an exact 6 grants one immediate bonus roll. Rolling three 6s in a row triggers an Engine Blowout, canceling that turn's movement.

Rule 3: Elevated Expressways (7 On-Ramps)

Landing on an on-ramp immediately launches the Harley forward: 4➔14 (+10), 9➔31 (+22), 21➔42 (+21), 28➔84 (+56 Mega Boost), 51➔67 (+16), 71➔91 (+20), 80➔97 (+17).

Rule 4: Red Pothole Portals & Emergency Repairs

Landing on a Red Pothole starts a 10-second timer. Pay ₹5 from your ₹10 reserve to hold position, or slide down the chute: 17➔7, 54➔34, 62➔19, 64➔60, 87➔24 (-63 Abyss), 93➔73, 95➔75, 99➔78.

Rule 5: Highway Side-Swipe Knockout

Landing on an occupied sector rear-ends the resident Harley, knocking it backward by 5 sectors. Secondary tunnels and potholes cascade automatically!
